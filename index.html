About

One calm window for the whole workday

Nova is a native macOS AI workspace that unifies a professional's entire workday in one calm window: email triage, calendar, projects, sourced research, voice, live media and scheduled AI agents. Unlike chat assistants, Nova acts — it prepares, drafts and organizes — but nothing is ever sent or executed without the user's explicit approval. It is model-agnostic by design, routing across Claude, OpenAI-compatible endpoints and Meta's Muse models through a production multi-model router. Nova was designed and built solo by a senior humanitarian analyst in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, who uses it every day for real, high-stakes analytical work at Save the Children. It has been accepted into the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026. Pre-revenue today, Nova's path to market runs through curated Mac distribution (Setapp), direct licensing and professional services.

The defining rule

"Nova prepares, drafts and organizes — but nothing is ever sent or executed without the user's explicit approval."

Every action Nova takes passes through a confirmation gate. It does the work; you keep the decision. That approval gate is the product's trust signature.

Technology

Native, model-agnostic, local-first

Native macOS (Swift)

A true native Mac application, built in Swift — not a web wrapper.

Multi-model router

A production multi-model failover router spanning 23 models keeps Nova working even when a provider fails.

Model-agnostic by design

Local-first
